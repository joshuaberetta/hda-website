Logo concepts

Five marks, four equal parts.

Each concept has four elements of equal weight, so no member leads. The four colours are fixed per concept but never assigned to specific organisations. Every mark is simple enough for a 16px favicon and quiet enough to sit next to four member logos.

Concepts for discussion. Final artwork would be redrawn and optically adjusted once a direction is chosen.
