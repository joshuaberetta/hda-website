03 Commitments

How we work together.

Collaboration creates greater value than any organisation can achieve alone.

  1. Coordination

    Strengthen coordination and reduce fragmentation across the humanitarian data ecosystem.

  2. Access to expertise

    Make specialised technical expertise more accessible where it is needed most.

  3. Interoperability

    Develop interoperable, demand-driven and fit-for-purpose services.

  4. Responsible data

    Promote responsible data stewardship and open knowledge.

  5. Local leadership

    Reinforce local expertise, leadership and long-term capacity.

  6. Practical innovation

    Pursue innovation that responds to real humanitarian challenges.

People will always come before data.

Every dataset, map, analysis and tool must ultimately serve a greater purpose: enabling faster, more informed and more accountable decisions for communities affected by crises.
